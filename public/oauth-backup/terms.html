SchoolCore Backup rclone

Nature of this integration

SchoolCore Backup rclone is a tool operated privately by the SchoolCore owner. It is intended only for authorised SchoolCore backup and recovery operations. It is not offered as a public service, and no public user accounts are provided through it.

No commercial Drive service

No commercial Google Drive service, storage product or third-party access is offered through this integration. The Google Drive account is used solely as private off-site storage for one deployment's encrypted backups.

Availability

Operation depends partly on third-party components, including Google Drive, rclone and Restic. Their availability and behaviour are outside the owner's control, and no availability guarantee is offered.

Owner responsibilities

  • Custody of the Restic repository recovery password.
  • Access control to the backup account and the self-hosted server.
  • Verification that restores succeed before relying on them.

Prohibited use

Unauthorised access to, disclosure of, or tampering with backup data is prohibited. The integration must not be used to reach content in the Drive account that it did not create.
